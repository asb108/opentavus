# Community conduct

OpenTavus welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages,
and ways of contributing. Treat people with respect. Discuss the work clearly and
help newcomers understand the project.

- Critique a proposed change and its evidence without attacking its author.
- Respect privacy, consent, identities, and boundaries. Do not post another person's
  face, voice, messages, credentials, or personal information without permission.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, and repeated unwanted contact.
- Give credit, disclose relevant conflicts, and describe real limitations honestly.
- Respect a maintainer's request to stop a disruptive discussion or move it elsewhere.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project
events, and interactions in which someone represents the project. Maintainers also
follow these rules.

Report a concern to the repository maintainer, [Atul / asb108](https://github.com/asb108),
using an available private contact on that profile. Do not publish sensitive details
in an issue. For a software vulnerability, use [the security process](SECURITY.md).
If private contact is unavailable, ask the maintainer for a private reporting channel
without describing the incident publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, ask for a correction, restrict
participation, or ban repeated/severe misconduct. They will consider context, explain
decisions to the affected person when feasible, protect reporters' privacy, and avoid
retaliation. A person may ask the maintainer to reconsider with new evidence.
