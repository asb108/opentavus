## Newton's Second Law of Motion

$$F = ma$$

Newton's second law states that the net force acting on an object is equal to its mass times its acceleration. This equation, F=ma, succinctly captures this principle.

## What is Newton's second law of motion?

1. F = ma
2. W = mg
3. E = mc^2
4. P = mv

Answer: F = ma. Newton's second law states that the net force acting on an object is equal to its mass times its acceleration. This can be mathematically expressed as F = ma.
